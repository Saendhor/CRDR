From your local machine (not the cluster), use `scp` or `rsync`:

```bash
# copies the folder contents into a local dir
scp -r username@leonardo:/leonardo/home/userexternal/spennisi/CRDR ./downloads/

# or rsync (resumable, better for big folders)
rsync -avP username@leonardo:/leonardo/home/userexternal/spennisi/CRDR/ ./downloads/
```

Note the trailing `/` on the source means "the contents of" rather than the folder itself. For very large transfers, tar the folder on the cluster first: `tar czf folder.tgz folder/`, then scp the single file.
